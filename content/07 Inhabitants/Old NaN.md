Though it seems obvious that Old NaN started out as some kind of defective [[RATs]] unit, NaN seems incapable of accepting as much. Regardless, over the years NaN has modified itself to remove anything that would identify its base model; its armor plating, limbs, chassis, headplate, and accessories are all custom to NaN, with one exception.

When asked who or what it is, NaN points to the only stock plate still on its body - the original identifying digits scratched out to be unreadable - and responds "Not a number." People have called it Old NaN for as long as anyone can remember.

NaN seems happy to scour safe areas of the city looking for salvage it can either resell or adapt to its own body.
