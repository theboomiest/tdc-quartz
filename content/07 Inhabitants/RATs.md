# Reclamation & Anti-Trespass units

The Reclamation & Anti Trespass units - RATs, are droids whose only apparent goal is to restore the city to its former glory. However, whatever schematics these robots are using - if any - are corrupted. They lay concrete and rebar in nonsensical patterns, creating labyrinthine dungeons that serve no purpose. These dungeons are commonly referred to as rats' nests and are typically avoided unless causing problems for a nearby settlement. 

The RATs themselves often have defects that cause unpredictable quirks in their base functions. This almost always includes unwarranted aggression against what the RATs consider trespassers. A notable exception is [[Old NaN]] in [[Dreg]], who does not seem to recognize itself as a RAT at all.

The RATs crop up randomly and their numbers never seem to dissipate no matter how many are taken out; there must be a factory pumping them out somewhere in [[The Dead City]]. 

## Builder RATs

Builder units are able to rapidly fashion structures out of raw materials provided by [[#Salvage RATs]]. Builders are slow but sturdy, and while not made for combat, can capably defend themselves.

## Combat RATs

![[img_RAT-combat.png]]

Functional combat units are rare in recent times, but records document swarms of them sweeping over the city between the first and second cataclysms. As a result, many inert combat units can be found throughout the city in various states of destruction.

The guns wielded by combat units seem uniquely powered by the combat unit itself, in that the ammunition contains no explosive or other means to propel the bullet. So far all attempts at replicating the power source have failed.
## Salvage RATs

Fragile and clumsy, Salvage units are hardly more than an electric furnace with limbs. They extract usable materials from scrap, either to repair damaged RATs or be used by [[#Builder RATs]] for construction.

## Scout RATs

![[img_RAT-scout.png]]

Scouts are the lightest and fastest of the typical RAT units. They scour the dead city for sizeable deposits of metal, out of which new nests and RAT units can be built by [[#Salvage RATs]]. Capable of climbing on all four limbs if the terrain is suitably rugged. Not particularly perceptive, Scouts' scanners are better at identifying inorganic material for building rather than spotting the shapes of trespassers.
