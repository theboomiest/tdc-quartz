## Core Beliefs

The Fellowship of Warranted Violence believes that the universe speaks to its inhabitants through violent acts, and therefore combat is a form of holy communion. Members believe that responding to violence with more violence is not only justified, but deific. There are guidelines to which other situations allow violence from the Fellowship members, but most cases can be simplified to: **Violence is Warranted against the formidable.**
