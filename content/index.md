## Summary
This is a fantasy setting inspired by Kenshi which takes place in a post-post-apocalyptic megacity. 

The [[First Cataclysm]] led survivors to shelter in the world's only surviving megacity. The survivors formed their own factions, religions, and cultures and brought the city's corpse some semblance of its former vibrancy. 

A [[Second Cataclysm]] followed some decades later, destroying much of what was rebuilt and returning civilization to the dark ages. The most horrific and awe-inspiring accounts of the two cataclysms serve as both factions' histories and the basis for magic in the world. Towns and outposts have sprung up in the carcass of the dead city in the last century; civilization crawling back from the brink. The vast expanse of crumbling ruins, toxic wastes, and blistered glasslands house innumerable threats - old and new.
