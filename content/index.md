## Summary
This is a fantasy setting inspired by Kenshi which takes place in a post-post apocalyptic megacity. Some initial planet-wide cataclysmic event led the survivors to shelter in the only remaining megacity. The survivors formed their own factions, religions, and cultures and brought the shell of the megacity some semblance of its former vibrancy. A second cataclysm followed some decades later, returning civilization to the dark ages. The most horrific and awe-inspiring retellings of the first cataclysm serve as both faction history and the basis for the setting's spellcasting systems. Towns and outposts have sprung up in the bones of the dead megacity in the last century; civilization crawling back from the brink. The vast expanse of crumbling ruins, toxic wastes, and blistered glasslands house innumerable threats - old and new.
