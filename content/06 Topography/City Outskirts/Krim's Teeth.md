North of [[The Dead City]] sits a low-lying mountain range topped with massive, jagged shards of glass and salt.

Occasionally, powerful storms form across the mountains and sweep across them, buffeting the city with violent winds imbued with shards of glass and salt.

The mountains are named after [[Krim Inkblot]], an orcish cartographer who made it his life's goal to map the extent of the mountain range. He died on his final expedition, but his body and completed map were recovered. Sadly, Krim's handwriting and artistry proved atrocious, and the map is largely useless. The mountain range was named Krim's Teeth in his honor.
