## Overview

This page should give you enough information to create a character in The Dead City. Other than the page for your character's upcoming quest, none of the other pages in this wiki are required reading.

## Dungeons & Dragons 5.5e

The game system is DnD 5.5e with all standard PHB options, equipment, spells, etc available for character creation. 

If some option exists in both 5e (2014) and 5.5e (2024), you must use the updated 5.5e option unless a DM has given specific permission otherwise. [There are backwards-compatibility rules for bringing the legacy 5e subclasses into 5.5e that are fairly straightforward](https://dungeonmister.com/guides/2014-character-options-with-2024-rules/#:~:text=D%26D%202024-,Some%20general%20conversion%20advice,-Aarakockra%3A%20Wizards%20of).

Setting-specific options are not allowed by default (Ghosts of Saltmarsh, Curse of Strahd, etc.), but if you feel an option is a natural fit to the setting and helps your character concept you may ask the DM for an exception.
